What do the factor scores mean?
Form balance
Compares each team's recent results (last 5 games). 50 = evenly matched. Higher = the stronger side has a clear form edge going in.
Momentum
Whether each team is trending up or down — winning streaks, improving performances, or a recent collapse heading into this game.
Goal tendency
Goals scored and conceded patterns. Reflects attacking threat and defensive vulnerability — useful for spotting high-scoring or tight games.
Context
Match importance, league position, tournament stage, and situational factors — rotation risk, cup pressure, must-win scenarios, etc.
